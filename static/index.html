FIRST WATCH · FIELD GUIDE

How to bring them home.

  1. Read your role's private intelligence. Clues are split across the crew.
  2. Talk, then place your order. The Navigator chooses a ship and route. Weather braces a passage. Reef Scout charts it. Keeper aims the beam.
  3. Line up the course and beam. Safe water and a braced storm passage get a ship home. A wrong turn costs a ship and lighthouse energy.
  4. Send an Echo Signal. Spend one shared charge to send one quick signal per watch.
  5. Rescue three before watch seven. Lose two ships and the storm wins.

Orders resolve when all active roles have acted, or when the 45-second watch timer expires.
